Why standard decks fail

The first reader is a model.

01

OCR & chart flattening

Ingestion pipelines miss metrics trapped in images, cap tables, and multi-column layouts. If the number is only in a PNG, it does not exist.

02

No narrative context

Slides are fragments. Humans supply the reasoning on the call. Standalone, an LLM flags incomplete reasoning as an incomplete business.

03

Adversarial evaluator prompts

Associates already ask: “Give me five reasons to pass. Find TAM discrepancies. List unaddressed regulatory risk.” Your deck is scored against that prompt.

Pass
