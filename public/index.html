The gaps are easy to miss when it’s your own deck

A good pitch can still leave big questions.

01

Great chart. Where are the numbers?

That chart took hours to get right. But if its numbers are trapped in an image, a text-based review can miss them. Put the figures where they can be read.

02

You won’t be there to explain slide seven.

You know why customers buy and how the business makes money. Does the deck explain it, or does it need you talking over it?

03

“Why should we pass?”

It’s an uncomfortable question. Ask it now. Test the market estimate, the margins, and the moat while you still have time to improve the answers.
