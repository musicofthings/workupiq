Gatekeeper Checklist

Before you send it, check these six things.

Can someone find the numbers? Can you back up the claims? Are the supporting documents ready? Give yourself one point for each honest yes on the full checklist.

Gate A

Can the text be read?

Make key figures selectable as text. Add captions and readable tables so a chart image isn’t the only source.

Gate B

Are the numbers easy to find?

State your market estimates, unit economics, funding ask, valuation, and runway clearly. Show the assumptions behind them.

Gate C

Can you back it up?

Link important claims to sources. Explain the basis of your regulatory plan and be specific about where competitors have an advantage.

Gate D

Have you answered the hard questions?

Explain how your edge works, what your costs include, and what you know about customer acquisition. Say when you don’t have the data yet.

Gate E

What would change your mind?

For scientific or clinical claims, state the intended use, sample size, and limitations. Describe the test that could prove your assumption wrong.

Gate F

Is the follow-up ready?

Prepare a short memo, a separate FAQ, an organized data-room index, and a concise email. Make the next step easy for the reader.
